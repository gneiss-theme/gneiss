Every color on this page comes from
[`palette.toml`](https://github.com/gneiss-theme/gneiss/blob/main/palette.toml)
when the site builds, and every contrast ratio is worked out from it. Ratios
are WCAG 2 contrast ratios.

## Base colors

The base colors are numbered like Nord's, so a port can be compared with
Nord's. The four background grays and the comment gray are Gneiss's own;
the text and accent colors are Nord's values.

<BaseColors />

## Contrast steps

The background grays keep Nord's steps: each is as far from the background
as its Nord counterpart is from Nord's. The comment gray is as light as
Nord's comment color. [Design](/design/) explains why.

<ContrastSteps />

## Roles

`palette.toml` also names roles, for apps themed from raw colors rather than
by a port of their own, such as a window manager's borders or a desktop
shell. Each role names a base color, except `fg-muted`.

<Roles />

## As data

- [`palette.toml`](https://github.com/gneiss-theme/gneiss/blob/main/palette.toml)
  has the base colors under `[base]` and the roles under `[colors]`.
- The Nix flake has it parsed, as `lib.palette`: see [Nix](/apps/nix/).
