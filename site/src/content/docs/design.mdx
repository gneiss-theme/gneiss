## Where it came from

Gneiss began in a personal NixOS configuration as Nord with neutral
backgrounds. Its colors started as a copy of
[Nord](https://www.nordtheme.com)'s values, and from there it's its own
theme: nothing follows Nord, so a change to Nord doesn't change Gneiss.

## The background grays

Nord's backgrounds, which it calls Polar Night, are blue-grays. Gneiss's are
neutral grays, each chosen to keep its Nord counterpart's contrast with the
background: `gneiss1` to `gneiss3` are 1.24, 1.45 and 1.70 to 1 against
`gneiss0`, and `nord1` to `nord3` are 1.24, 1.45 and 1.69 to 1 against
`nord0`. So panels, the current
line, selections and borders stand apart from the background as much as they
do in Nord. The background itself, `gneiss0`, is darker than `nord0`.

[The palette page](/palette/#contrast-steps) works these ratios out
from the colors, and the repo's checks fail if they drift.

## The comment gray

Nord's comment color, `#616E88`, is a lighter blue-gray. `gneiss-comment`,
`#6E6E6E`, is a neutral gray as light as it (their relative luminance is
within 2%), so comments recede as far as they do in Nord.

## Text and accents

The text colors (`gneiss4` to `gneiss6`) and the accents (`gneiss7` to
`gneiss15`) are Nord's values. The cool accents carry most of the syntax:
functions, keywords and types. The warm ones keep their meaning: red for
errors and deletions, yellow for warnings and green for strings and
additions.

## fg-muted

`fg-muted`, `#A2A2A2`, is the one role that isn't a base color. It's a gray
for secondary text that stays readable, at least 4.5:1, on `gneiss2`, the
gray of selections and raised controls. Nord has no color for this.

## Changes from Nord in the ports

The VS Code theme began as a fork of
[Nord's VS Code theme](https://github.com/nordtheme/visual-studio-code), and
the Neovim colorscheme as a fork of
[nord.nvim](https://github.com/gbprod/nord.nvim). Both make the same choice
of color for each part of the editor as their originals, with Gneiss's
colors in place of Nord's. Where the ports differ from Nord:

- Terminal bright black (color 8) is the comment gray. Terminal programs dim
  text with it, and Nord's border gray, which Nord uses there, is too faint
  to read.
- Vicinae's accent is `gneiss8`, where Vicinae's own Nord theme uses
  `nord9`, and its border, selection and grid grays are all `gneiss3`.

## Versions

Gneiss is 0.x while its colors may still change, and every color change is
listed in the
[changelog](https://github.com/gneiss-theme/gneiss/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
It reaches 1.0 once they've settled.
